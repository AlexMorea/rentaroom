## 1. Listing is free
1.1 Creating an account and listing rooms on Rooms4You is free, with no
limit on the number of rooms.
1.2 Rooms4You does not charge landlords a success fee, placement fee or
commission on rent, and does not charge tenants to search for or contact
landlords.

## 2. Optional paid services
2.1 **Pro** is an optional subscription. Its price depends on the number of
rooms you have listed (a listing with several identical units counts each
unit) and is shown on the Plans page before you pay.
2.2 **Boosts** are optional once-off purchases that show one listing as
"Featured" near the top of relevant search results for the period bought.
A boost does not guarantee enquiries or tenants. Only listings with a
vacancy that have been recently confirmed as accurate can be boosted.
2.3 Paid services never affect verification. The Verified badge is awarded
only after Rooms4You's own checks and cannot be bought.

## 3. Payment and renewal
3.1 Payments are processed by PayFast. Rooms4You does not store card details.
3.2 Pro renews automatically each month or year (as chosen) until cancelled.
3.3 You can cancel Pro at any time on the Plans page. Cancelling stops
future payments; Pro continues until the end of the period already paid
for, after which your account moves to the free plan. Your listings are not
removed.
3.4 We will give at least 30 days' notice by email before changing the
price of an active subscription.

## 4. Founding Landlords
4.1 Landlords who registered on or before 31 January 2027 receive Pro free
until 30 April 2027.
4.2 After that, Founding Landlords may subscribe to Pro at 50% off the
standard price for as long as the subscription stays active. If a paid
subscription ends and is not renewed within 30 days, the founding discount
ends.

## 5. Refunds
5.1 [Lawyer to confirm, incl. ECTA cooling-off] Boosts start immediately
and are not refundable once started, except where required by law.
5.2 [Lawyer to confirm] Pro payments are not refunded for partial periods,
except where required by law.

## 6. Suspension
6.1 Rooms4You may suspend an account for fraud, misleading listings or
other breaches of these terms. Suspension is never used to collect payment.
