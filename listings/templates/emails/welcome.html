Getting your first room seen

  1. Add your room with the price, what's included and the area. Tenants see the suburb, never your street address.
  2. Upload clear photos of the room, the bathroom and the entrance. Listings with good photos get far more enquiries.
  3. Reply quickly. Your average reply time shows on your profile, and fast replies earn a "Fast responder" badge.
  4. Confirm your listing every two weeks when we remind you, so tenants know the room is really available.

Keeping yourself safe

Arrange viewings at a time when someone else is home, and ask to see a tenant's ID before you sign a lease or hand over keys.
