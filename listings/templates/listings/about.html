About Rooms4You

Rooms4You is a South African rental platform built for township and affordable housing communities - a sector that has long relied on informal channels like Facebook groups, WhatsApp, and word of mouth.

These channels often expose tenants to scams, fake listings, and unsafe transactions. At the same time, landlords struggle to find serious tenants and fill vacancies efficiently.

Our mission

To bring trust, technology, and convenience to South Africa’s informal rental economy - making affordable housing and room discovery safer, faster, and more transparent for everyone.

How we build trust

  • Verified landlord profiles reviewed by our admin team
  • Landlord identity vetting before trusted badges are awarded
  • Verified listing badges to help tenants avoid scams
  • Tenant safety tools like Guardian4You for added peace of mind
  • Reviews and ratings that create accountability

What we believe

Affordable housing deserves the same level of technology and trust as premium property markets.

Rooms4You is not just a listing platform - we are building the trust infrastructure for South Africa’s informal rental economy.

Meet The Founders

Rooms4You was founded by Alex 'MathMetiX' Morea and Jacob Bogopa, two South Africans committed to solving one of the country's most persistent housing challenges.

After recognizing the risks and frustrations many people face when searching for accommodation through informal channels, they set out to create a safer and more trusted way for tenants and landlords to connect.

Today, Rooms4You is building the trust infrastructure for South Africa's informal rental economy through technology, verification, transparency, and community-driven growth.
