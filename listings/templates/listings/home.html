WHAT IS ROOMS4YOU?

A Simpler Way To Rent

Rooms4You is a South African property technology (PropTech) platform that connects tenants with landlords through a trusted, simple, and secure digital rental marketplace.

THE CHALLENGE

The Problem We're Solving

Finding a room in South Africa's informal rental market is harder and riskier than it should be.

🚨

Rental scams that cost tenants money before they've even seen a room.

🎭

Fake listings for rooms that don't exist or aren't actually available.

🔍

Difficulty finding affordable rooms without endless scrolling and dead ends.

🏚️

No trusted platform focused specifically on affordable accommodation.

THE SOLUTION

How Rooms4You Solves It

Built from the ground up to remove the risk and hassle from renting.

✅

Verified Landlords

💬

Secure Messaging

🔎

Easy Room Discovery

Search and filter by location, price, and room type in seconds.

🚩

Report Suspicious Listings

Affordable Housing Focus

TRUST & SAFETY

Why South Africans Trust Rooms4You

Safety isn't an afterthought here — it's built into how the platform works.

✓ Email Verification

Every account confirms a real email address with a one-time code before it can list or message.

✓ Reporting & Moderation

Flag a suspicious listing or user in a couple of taps — reports are reviewed by our team, not left unaddressed.

✓ Safer Viewings

Guardian4You helps users stay safer when meeting landlords in person.

✓ Direct Communication

Connect directly with property owners and reduce unnecessary middlemen.

✓ We Only Get Paid When You Move In

Landlords don't pay us to post a listing. Our success fee is only invoiced once both the tenant and landlord confirm a real move-in — so we're only rewarded for a placement that actually happened.

⚡ Real Response Times

See how fast a landlord actually replies, measured from real conversations — not a claim, a track record.

🔄 Availability You Can Trust

Landlords confirm their listings are still accurate on a schedule — stale ones get flagged, then hidden, instead of sitting there indefinitely.
