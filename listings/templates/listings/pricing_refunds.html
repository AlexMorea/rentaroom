1. Free for everyone

  • Tenants never pay to search, view listings or contact a landlord on Rooms4You.
  • Landlords list for free, with no limit on the number of rooms.
  • We do not charge a success fee, placement fee or commission on rent, and we never collect rent or deposits.

The paid services below are optional extras for verified landlords who want to fill rooms faster. Only landlords who have passed our free verification can buy them, so scammers can't pay to be seen.

A Boost shows one listing near the top of matching search results, clearly labelled "Featured", for the period bought. It's a once-off payment, not a subscription. A Boost increases visibility; it does not guarantee enquiries or a tenant. Only listings with a vacancy that have been recently confirmed as accurate can be boosted.

Paid services never affect verification: the Verified badge is awarded only after our own checks and can't be bought.

4. Payment & security

  • All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR) and are the full amount you pay{% if COMPANY.vat_number %}, including VAT{% endif %}. There are no setup or hidden fees.
  • Payments are processed securely by PayFast, a licensed South African payment provider. You can pay by credit or debit card, Instant EFT, and other methods PayFast offers at checkout.
  • Card details are entered on PayFast's secure page. Rooms4You never sees or stores your card details. We keep only a record of the payment (amount, date and PayFast reference).
  • Pro subscriptions are collected automatically by PayFast each month or year on the same date, until you cancel. PayFast emails you a receipt for every payment.
  • We'll give at least 30 days' notice by email before changing the price of an active subscription.

5. When you get access

Pro and Boosts are digital services with nothing to post or collect. They switch on automatically as soon as PayFast confirms your payment, usually within a minute. If a service isn't active within an hour of paying, email {{ COMPANY.billing_email }} with your payment reference and we'll sort it out.

6. Cancelling

  • You can cancel Pro at any time from the Plans page (or the link in any PayFast subscription email). There's no contract and no cancellation fee.
  • Cancelling stops future payments. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for, then your account moves to the free plan.
  • Your listings are never removed because a subscription ends.
  • Boosts are once-off and end automatically; there's nothing to cancel.

7. Refunds

  • Charged in error or service not delivered: if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or a paid service didn't switch on and we can't fix it, we'll refund the payment in full. Contact us within 14 days of the payment.
  • Pro: payments for a period that has already started aren't refunded for the unused part, but you can cancel at any time so you're never charged again.
  • Boosts: a Boost starts immediately when you buy it, so it isn't refundable once it has started, unless it was charged in error or didn't work.
  • Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.
  • Approved refunds are paid back through PayFast to the original payment method, normally within 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank.

8. Billing questions & complaints

Email {{ COMPANY.billing_email }} with your name, the email on your Rooms4You account and the payment reference. We aim to reply within 2 working days and to resolve billing complaints within 10 working days. If you're not happy with the outcome, you can contact the National Consumer Commission or the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud.
