Privacy Policy

At Rooms4You, operated by 4You (Pty) Ltd, we respect your privacy and comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Profile and listing information
  • Messages between users
  • Location data (if enabled)
  • Uploaded images and documents

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To display listings and enable platform functionality
  • To improve safety and detect fraud
  • To send important service notifications

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is only shared when required to operate the platform (e.g. hosting, email services) or when legally required.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using trusted third-party service providers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account (subject to legal obligations)

6. Security

We implement security measures to reduce unauthorised access, but no system is 100% secure.
