Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ COMPANY.legal_last_updated|date:"j F Y" }}

At {{ COMPANY.trading_name }}, operated by {{ COMPANY.legal_name }}, we respect your privacy and process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Full name, email address and phone number
  • Account credentials (passwords are stored encrypted, never in plain text)
  • Profile and listing information, including photos you upload
  • Messages between users on the platform
  • Location data, only if you enable it (for example Guardian safety mode)
  • Documents you upload for verification
  • Payment records for paid services: amount, date, what was bought and the PayFast reference. We never receive or store card or bank details; those are entered on PayFast's secure page.
  • Basic technical data such as device and browser type and pages visited, used for security and to improve the service

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage accounts
  • To show listings and connect tenants with landlords
  • To provide paid services, confirm payments and keep financial records
  • To keep the platform safe: verifying landlords, detecting fraud and handling reports
  • To send service messages, such as enquiry alerts, listing reminders and payment receipts

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers we need to run Rooms4You, or when the law requires it:

  • PayFast: processes payments for Pro and Boosts
  • Hosting and database providers: run the website and store data
  • Cloudinary: stores listing and profile photos
  • Brevo: sends our emails
  • Google: maps, site analytics, and sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google"

Some providers store data outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard comparable to POPIA.

Other users see what you choose to publish, such as your listing, first name and public profile. A landlord's full street address is not shown publicly.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and protect forms, and remember preferences such as dark mode. We also use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you close your account we delete or anonymise it, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records, which are kept for at least 5 years for tax purposes, and information needed to deal with fraud reports or disputes.

6. Your rights

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete inaccurate information
  • Close your account and ask us to delete your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Object to direct marketing at any time

Email {{ COMPANY.privacy_email }} and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, access controls and a licensed payment provider to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we'll notify you and the Information Regulator if a breach puts your information at risk.
