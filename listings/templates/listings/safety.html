Safety Tips

Your safety matters. Rooms4You was built to reduce scams and help tenants rent with confidence.

  • Never pay before viewing. If someone pressures you for money first, report them immediately.
  • Prefer renting from Verified Landlords whenever possible.
  • Meet during daylight and bring a trusted friend or family member.
  • Confirm landlord identity and compare details carefully.
  • Use Rooms4You contact tools (Call / WhatsApp / Email) so suspicious behaviour can be detected.
  • Use Guardian4You when visiting unfamiliar locations for extra peace of mind.
  • Report duplicate listings, spam, or scams - our team reviews all reports.

Safe tenants build stronger communities.
