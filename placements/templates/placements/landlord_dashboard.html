How the Success Fee works

Rooms4You is free to list on. We only charge a once-off Success Fee after a placement actually works out - both you and the tenant confirm they've moved in. No successful placement, no fee.

Rent R800 – R1,500 R100
Rent R1,501 – R2,500 R150
Rent R2,501 – R4,000 R250
Rent above R4,000 R350

Why we charge it: it's what keeps Rooms4You free to list on, lets us keep verifying landlords and moderating listings, and keeps the platform focused on actually placing tenants - not just collecting listing fees whether or not anyone moves in.

If a Success Fee isn't paid: you'll get a reminder first. If it's still unpaid after a grace period, your account is suspended - you won't be able to add new listings until it's settled. Existing listings and your history aren't deleted, and reactivating is as simple as clearing the outstanding fee.
