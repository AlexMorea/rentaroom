Shaya: made in Mzansi, paid in Mzansi

Shaya is a short-video app built in South Africa for South African creators. TikTok's Creator Rewards Programme still isn't available in South Africa, so local creators who go viral often earn nothing from the views. Shaya pays them.

How creators get paid

  1. Local businesses buy sponsored clips in the feed.
  2. Every month, {{ creator_share_pct|floatformat:0 }}% of that ad revenue, plus any sponsor or partner top-ups, goes into the creator pool.
  3. The pool is shared by paid views: real views (watched for at least 3 seconds, not your own, one per person per day) on clips that have reached {{ threshold }} views.
  4. Withdraw to any SA bank with PayShap, from R{{ min_payout_rands|floatformat:0 }}.

The rate per 1,000 views moves with ad sales, so we publish it every month instead of promising a fixed number.

Built for SA data costs

Data saver is on by default: videos play from a lighter ~480p copy and don't download until you scroll to them.

Safety

Anyone can report a clip. Reports are reviewed by people, in line with the Films and Publications Act and POPIA. Under-18s can post but can't join the paid programme.
