Fraud Alerts

Stay informed about common scams, suspicious activity and important safety notices affecting the Rooms4You community. We update this page whenever new scam trends are identified.

🟢 Current Platform Status

There are currently no known security breaches affecting Rooms4You.

However, scammers may still attempt to impersonate landlords, tenants or even Rooms4You staff. Staying informed is your best protection.

ACTIVE ALERT

Fake Deposit Requests

Last Updated: July 2026

We've received reports of scammers asking tenants to pay deposits before viewing properties. In many cases the scammer disappears immediately after receiving payment.

How to Stay Safe

  • ✔ View the property whenever possible.
  • ✔ Verify the landlord.
  • ✔ Don't rush into making payment.
  • ✔ Contact us if something seems suspicious.

Common Rental Scams

💳 Deposit Scam

A scammer asks for money before you can inspect the property.

🏠 Fake Property

Photos are stolen from another website and used to advertise a room that doesn't exist.

📱 WhatsApp Impersonation

Someone pretends to represent Rooms4You or the landlord using a different phone number.

📧 Fake Emails

Fraudsters send emails pretending to be Rooms4You using similar email addresses.

🚩 What Should You Do?

  1. Stop communicating if something feels suspicious.
  2. Do not send money until you're comfortable.
  3. Take screenshots of messages.
  4. Save email addresses and phone numbers.
  5. Report the incident to Rooms4You.

🔒 Safety Reminders

  • ✔ Rooms4You never asks tenants to send rental deposits to us.
  • ✔ We never ask for your password.
  • ✔ We never ask for your OTP.
  • ✔ Always verify official communication.
  • ✔ Report suspicious behaviour immediately.
