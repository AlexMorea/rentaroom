Our Trust Promise

Rooms4You was created to make finding rental accommodation safer, easier and more transparent. We continuously improve our platform, educate our community about scams, investigate reports of fraud, and verify landlords where possible. While no online marketplace can completely eliminate fraud, informed users are far less likely to become victims. Together, we can build South Africa's safest township rental marketplace.

🚨 Current Safety Alert

Some scammers pretend to be landlords and ask tenants to pay deposits before viewing properties. Rooms4You does not collect rental deposits. Always inspect the property, verify the landlord, and never rush into making payment.

✔ Before You Pay Any Landlord

  • ✔ Visited the property where possible
  • ✔ Confirmed the address
  • ✔ Verified the landlord
  • ✔ Checked listing photos carefully
  • ✔ Asked questions
  • ✔ Verified payment details
  • ✔ Trust your instincts if something feels wrong
