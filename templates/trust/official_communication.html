How to Know It's Really Us

Unfortunately, scammers sometimes pretend to represent trusted companies. They may copy our logo, send fake emails, create fake social media pages, or request payments while pretending to be Rooms4You. This page explains exactly how we communicate with our community.

❌ Rooms4You Will Never...

  • Ask for your password
  • Ask for your banking PIN
