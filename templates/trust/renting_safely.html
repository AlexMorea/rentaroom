Rent With Confidence

Finding the right room is exciting, but taking a few simple precautions can protect your money, your personal information, and your peace of mind. Use this guide before agreeing to rent any property.

1️⃣ Research the Listing

  • ✔ Read the entire listing carefully.
  • ✔ Check the monthly rent and any additional costs.
  • ✔ Review all available photos.
  • ✔ Make sure the description matches the photos.
  • ✔ If something seems unclear, ask questions before proceeding.

2️⃣ Verify the Landlord

Before paying or signing anything, make sure you're dealing with the correct person.

  • ✔ Check whether the landlord has a Verified badge.
  • ✔ Confirm their contact details.
  • ✔ Ask who owns or manages the property.
  • ✔ Don't ignore warning signs or inconsistent answers.

3️⃣ View the Property

Whenever possible, inspect the property before making any payment.

  • ✔ Confirm the address.
  • ✔ Check that the room matches the photos.
  • ✔ Inspect doors, windows, plumbing and electricity.
  • ✔ Ask about house rules, visitors and shared spaces.
  • ✔ Meet the landlord or property manager.

4️⃣ Understand the Costs

Make sure you know exactly what you're expected to pay.

  • ✔ Monthly rent.
  • ✔ Deposit (if applicable).
  • ✔ Electricity or water charges.
  • ✔ Parking fees.
  • ✔ Any once-off administration fees.

5️⃣ Before You Pay

Take a moment to complete this checklist.
  • ☐ I have viewed the property or made reasonable arrangements to verify it.
  • ☐ I know who I am paying.
  • ☐ I understand what the payment covers.
  • ☐ I have asked all my questions.
  • ☐ I feel comfortable proceeding.

6️⃣ Keep Records

Keep copies of important information throughout the rental process.

  • ✔ Payment confirmations.
  • ✔ Chat messages.
  • ✔ Emails.
  • ✔ Rental agreements.
  • ✔ Photos taken during your viewing.

🛡️ Renting on Rooms4You Today

At the moment, Rooms4You helps tenants and landlords connect. Rental payments are made directly between tenants and landlords. We do not currently collect or hold rental deposits.

We're actively working on additional trust and safety features, including enhanced landlord verification and secure payment options, to make renting even safer in the future.

🚩 If Something Doesn't Feel Right

If you feel pressured, notice inconsistent information, or suspect fraudulent activity, pause the process. Take time to verify the details before making any payment or sharing personal information.
