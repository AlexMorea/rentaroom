Understanding Verification

Verification helps build confidence between landlords and tenants. It gives users additional information to help them make informed decisions, but it does not replace common sense or careful decision-making.

🏅 What Does "Verified" Mean?

A Verified badge means the landlord has successfully completed the current Rooms4You verification process.

Verification is one of several signals you should consider when deciding whether to rent a property. We still encourage tenants to inspect the property, ask questions, and verify important details before making any payment.

✅ Available Today

At launch, landlords who successfully complete our verification checks will receive a:

✔ VERIFIED LANDLORD

This badge helps tenants quickly identify landlords who have completed our current verification process.

🤝 Why Verification Matters

For Tenants

  • Greater confidence when searching.
  • Better transparency.
  • Easier comparison between listings.
  • Additional peace of mind.

For Landlords

  • Build trust faster.
  • Increase tenant confidence.
  • Stand out in search results.
  • Improve enquiry quality.

⚠ Important to Know

Verification is designed to improve trust, but it is not a guarantee.

  • ✔ Continue asking questions.
  • ✔ View the property whenever possible.
  • ✔ Confirm payment details carefully.
  • ✔ Read the listing thoroughly.
  • ✔ Report suspicious behaviour.

🏆 How Landlords Can Become Verified

  1. Create a Rooms4You account.
  2. Complete your landlord profile.
  3. Submit the requested verification information.
  4. Wait for our review.
  5. Receive your Verified badge once approved.
